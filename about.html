About Us

A new firm, built on experience that isn't.

Novon Technologies is a young consultancy — but the team behind it has spent years solving these problems inside large organizations first.

Our Story

Why we started Novon Technologies.

Novon Technologies was founded to give growing businesses the kind of senior, hands-on technology guidance that's usually reserved for large enterprise engagements — without the overhead of a big consultancy.

We're a new firm. We don't have a long client list yet, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we do have is a founder and team with real, hands-on delivery experience gained inside large organizations, now applied directly to your business instead of buried under layers of account management.

Our Founder

Led by hands-on technical experience.

Novon Technologies is led by our Founder, who brings over eight years of hands-on enterprise technology consulting experience across financial services, insurance, and aviation, with expertise spanning platform administration, custom application development, systems integration, DevOps, and applied AI.

This is backed by multiple professional certifications and a Master's degree in Project Management. Backed by a team of dedicated consultants who bring the same hands-on delivery discipline to every engagement, that combination of deep technical certification and formal delivery training is what lets us pair technical depth with well-managed execution, even as a new firm.

How We Work

A straightforward, three-stage approach.

No matter the size of the engagement, every project moves through the same disciplined process.

Discovery

We start by understanding your business, your current systems, and where the friction actually is — before proposing anything.

Roadmap

You get a clear, sequenced plan with defined scope, timeline, and outcomes — agreed before any work begins.

Delivery

Senior consultants do the actual work, with regular check-ins so you always know where things stand.

Our Values

What guides how we take on work.

Transparency

Honest scoping, honest pricing, and honest updates — including telling you when something isn't a fit.

Technical Excellence

Certified, hands-on expertise applied to your systems — not junior staff learning on your project.

Client Partnership

We work as an extension of your team, not a vendor handing off a deliverable and disappearing.
